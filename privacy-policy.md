# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** May 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how DismissTicket.com ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you visit our website at [https://dismissticket.com](https://dismissticket.com). We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our data practices.

---

## Information We Collect

### Information Collected Automatically

When you visit DismissTicket.com, we automatically collect certain information through standard web analytics, including:

- **Page views and browsing activity** — which pages you visit, how long you spend on each page, and your navigation path through the site.
- **Referrer information** — the website or search engine that directed you to our site.
- **Device and browser information** — your browser type, operating system, screen resolution, and device category (desktop, tablet, or mobile).
- **General location** — your approximate geographic location based on your IP address (country, state, or city level only).
- **Date and time of visit** — when you accessed our site.

This information is collected in aggregate and is used to understand how visitors use our site, improve our content, and ensure our website functions properly.

### Information We Do Not Collect

DismissTicket.com does **not** collect personal information from visitors. Specifically:

- We do not require user accounts or registration.
- We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or mailing addresses through the website.
- We do not collect payment information.
- We do not collect driver's license numbers, ticket numbers, or any details about your traffic citation.
- We do not have contact forms, comment sections, or other features that collect personally identifiable information (PII) through the website.

If you choose to email us at info@dismissticket.com, your email address and the contents of your message will be used solely to respond to your inquiry.

---

## Cookies

DismissTicket.com uses cookies for the following purposes:

- **Analytics cookies** — We use web analytics tools to understand site usage patterns, page popularity, and traffic sources. These cookies help us improve our content and user experience.
- **Essential cookies** — Cookies that are necessary for basic site functionality.

We do not use advertising cookies, retargeting cookies, or tracking pixels. We do not build user profiles or track individual visitors across other websites.

Most web browsers allow you to manage or block cookies through your browser settings. Blocking cookies may affect your experience on our site but will not prevent you from accessing our content.

## Third-Party Links

DismissTicket.com contains links to third-party websites, including:

- **Traffic school and defensive driving course providers** — Some of these links are affiliate links. When you click an affiliate link and enroll in a course, we may earn a commission at no additional cost to you.
- **Official government resources** — Links to state DMV websites, court websites, and legislative databases for verifying traffic laws and procedures.

When you click a link to a third-party website, you leave DismissTicket.com and are subject to that website's privacy policy and terms. We do not control the privacy practices of third-party websites and are not responsible for their content or data collection practices. We encourage you to review the privacy policy of any third-party website before providing your personal information.

---

## How We Use Information

The aggregated, non-personal information we collect through analytics is used to:

- Understand which state guides and tools are most useful to visitors.
- Identify technical issues or broken pages.
- Improve existing content and prioritize new content.
- Analyze traffic sources to understand how visitors find our site.

We do not use any collected information for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to operating and improving DismissTicket.com.

---

## Data Sharing and Sale

**We do not sell, rent, or trade any personal information.** We do not share visitor data with third parties for their marketing purposes.

Aggregated, non-identifying analytics data (such as total page views or most-visited pages) may be used internally but is not shared with or sold to third parties.

---

## California Residents (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) provides you with specific rights regarding your personal information. Because DismissTicket.com does not collect, sell, or share personal information as defined under the CCPA, these provisions have limited applicability to our site. However, for clarity:

- **Right to know:** We do not collect personal information beyond standard analytics data.
- **Right to delete:** We do not maintain personal information linked to individual visitors.
- **Right to opt out of sale:** We do not sell personal information to any third party.
- **Non-discrimination:** We do not discriminate against users who exercise their privacy rights.

If you have questions about your rights under the CCPA, contact us at [info@dismissticket.com](mailto:info@dismissticket.com).
